- exact model-family policy by position and population;
- feature list and transformations;
- target-level fallback rules;

- eligible-player/identity rules;

- scoring/reference metric definitions;
- MAE, RMSE, Spearman and top-N evaluation definitions;

The 2026 test must compare the same eligible population under:

1. transparent historical baseline;
2. validated v0.4 control;
3. any pre-registered Cycle-2 successor.

Report QB/RB/WR/TE separately and pooled, plus rookie and history-depth subgroups. Failed positions/seasons must remain visible.
