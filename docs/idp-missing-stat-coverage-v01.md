## Threshold bonuses

The probability models prove real signal but not point-projection readiness. For 2+ sack games, Brier improves from 0.0873 to 0.0711 across 2020-2024 and wins every fold, but expected qualifying-game count MAE is about 0.2569 versus 0.1571 for an always-zero count forecast. For 3+ PD games, Brier improves from 0.0506 to 0.0456 in all five folds, while expected-count MAE is about 0.1447 versus 0.0662 for zero.

These models may later support uncertainty or a better count-distribution model. They do not currently justify fantasy-point additions. Naive thresholding of fractional projected sacks or passes defended remains invalid.

## Sack yards

Sack yards are the largest measurable missing category under Founder-like weights. Historical mean contribution is about 1.53 points per DL season, 1.04 per LB season and 0.17 per DB season at the league's 0.1 point/yard setting.

For DL and LB, prior sacks, prior sack yards, prior games and prior solo-tackle volume support a stable chronology-safe Ridge expectation. DL improves MAE from about 16.56 to 12.90 and LB from 12.23 to 10.11, with the Ridge model beating zero in all five 2020-2024 development folds for both positions. Sack yards per sack are also comparatively stable by position.

DB fails the MAE gate: zero is about 1.89 MAE versus Ridge 2.73. Ridge does improve DB RMSE from about 5.54 to 4.93 and provides nonzero rank signal (~0.29 Spearman), but that tradeoff is insufficient to claim DB support. DB therefore remains explicitly unsupported; Core must not apply the DL/LB model or silently zero-fill the category.

## Return yards and sparse events

Interception-return yards are historically available and material for DBs, but occurrence dominates the problem. The tested models improve some RMSE/rank measures while losing MAE to zero. A future model should project interception count/probability first and then conditional return yards per interception.

Fumble-return yards are less frequent and less stable. Zero wins MAE at every IDP position. Fumble-recovery TDs, blocked kicks and special-teams TDs also lack enough player-level persistence for a defensible individual projection. `st_ff` and `st_fum_rec` are absent as separate Special Teams Player fields in the frozen weekly schema and cannot be inferred from defensive fields.

## Founder-like scoring impact

Across 2015-2024 player-seasons, average historical contributions for the eight observable unsupported categories are approximately:

1. `idp_sack_yd` ~0.84 points/player-season
2. `idp_int_ret_yd` ~0.55
3. `bonus_sack_2p` ~0.27
4. `idp_pass_def_3p` ~0.15
5. `fum_rec_td` ~0.14
6. `idp_fum_ret_yd` ~0.14
7. `idp_blk_kick` ~0.12
8. `st_td` ~0.03

`st_ff` and `st_fum_rec` cannot be estimated safely from this schema. Observed supported-only versus supported-plus-all-eight-observable scoring preserves >0.999 average rank correlation by position, with top-24 overlap generally 22-24 players. These categories modestly perturb ordering rather than dominate it, but nonzero unsupported settings must still block exact scoring.

## Coverage consequence

Current validated PR #30 player-key coverage remains **12/22 = 54.5%** globally. A QA PASS for the DL/LB sack-yard component would add only partial-position experimental support; it does not make `idp_sack_yd` globally supported because DB still lacks a validated model. The Founder-like league remains fail-closed and not exactly rankable.

## Frozen candidate contract

The only v0.1 candidate is `idp_sack_yd` for **DL and LB only**:

- model family: Ridge, alpha 10;
- target: season individual defensive sack yards;
- chronology: target season Y uses only seasons before Y;
- features: prior sacks, prior sack yards, prior games, prior solo-tackle volume;
- position scope: DL/LB supported by research candidate; DB unsupported;
- finite nonnegative prediction sanitation only after model output;
- no zero fallback for missing/unavailable source state;
- exact source snapshot SHA-256 and model version required;
- 2025 remains retrospective observed evidence only;
- `experimental=true`;
- `production_projection_eligible=false`;
- `idp_dynasty_value_available=false`;
- `dynasty_value=null`.

PR #30's completeness gate remains unchanged. Even if QA passes this partial-position component, the full Founder-like scoring shape remains blocked.

## Readiness

`idp_sack_yd` for **DL/LB only** is ready for independent HIGH-risk QA as a contained research component. The DB sack-yard model and the other nine categories are not ready.
